# Privacy Policy — Ludo

_Last updated: 2026-11-17_

## Summary

This app has no user accounts or developer-operated server, and the game runs on your device. It displays **advertisements through Google AdMob**. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google may collect and share data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, as described below.

## Data the app stores on your device

The app saves the following to your phone's local storage, only to run the game:

- **Game state**: the in-progress game (token positions, dice value, whose turn it is) so you can resume after closing the app.
- **Settings**: your preferences (sound on/off, AI difficulty, player colors, team mode).

This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data from your phone settings deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses **Google AdMob** to show ads (a banner and an occasional full-screen ad at the end of a game). To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process:

- **Device and account identifiers**, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and other account-related identifiers where applicable.
- **IP address**, which may be used to estimate general location, and device information such as model, OS version, and language.
- **App and ad interactions**, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
- **Diagnostic and performance information**, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.

Google reports that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. The SDK's data practices can change; review Google's [current Play data disclosure for AdMob](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure) when preparing or updating the Play Data safety form.

This data is handled by Google, not by us, under:

- Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time via your device: **Settings → Google → Ads**.

In some regions, the app shows Google's consent form for ad privacy choices. You can reopen available choices in the app under **Settings → Ad Privacy Options**.

Showing ads requires an **internet connection**; the rest of the game works offline.

## Data the game itself does NOT collect

Apart from the AdMob SDK data listed above, the game itself does not collect or transmit:

- Your name, email, or phone number.
- Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
- Data through a separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

We (the developer) do not receive any personal data from you — ad data is processed by Google.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — required to load ads.
- **Advertising ID** (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`) — used by Google Mobile Ads.

The app requests no other special permissions.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed for children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Google AdMob may process ad-related data as described in the advertising section above.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this file will reflect that. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact details on the Ludo Google Play Store listing.
